From Business Need to Successful Delivery
We do not begin with a list of available professionals. We begin by understanding what the client needs to achieve.
The Delivery Journey
1. Understand
We define the business challenge, outputs, required skills, timeline, budget, working arrangement and success measures.
2. Design
We recommend a defined project, dedicated professional, managed team, outsourced function or direct placement.
3. Source
We identify professionals whose skills, experience, availability and interests align with the assignment.
4. Assess
Assessment may include profile verification, portfolio review, technical tests, practical case studies, interviews and communication checks.
5. Match and Onboard
We present suitable talent, confirm the selection and prepare the professional or team for the client’s systems, expectations and milestones.
6. Deliver and Manage
Talent delivers against agreed outputs. Depending on the model, SasaKazi supports progress tracking, communication, quality review and issue escalation.
7. Review and Grow
We review performance and outcomes. Successful engagements can progress into new projects, dedicated teams, managed services or employment.
What Clients Can Expect
A documented requirement and recommended delivery model
Talent profiles aligned with the required capabilities
Clear onboarding and communication arrangements
Defined milestones and acceptance criteria
Progress reporting, feedback and escalation procedures
A close-out review and next-step recommendations
Quality Assurance
Quality is supported through clear scopes, relevant assessment, structured onboarding, defined milestones, progress reviews, client feedback and documented acceptance criteria.
Professional Standards
SasaKazi professionals must provide accurate information, protect client data, communicate honestly, meet deadlines, produce original work, respect intellectual property and maintain professional conduct.
Clients must provide clear assignments, timely access to information and feedback, agreed payment, and a safe, respectful and inclusive working environment.

4. About
SEO Title
About SasaKazi | Our Story, Mission and Impact
Meta Description
Discover how SasaKazi evolved from a digital-apprenticeship programme into an African digital talent and BPO company.
Page Banner
Africa Does Not Lack Talent. It Needs Stronger Pathways to Opportunity.
Our Story
SasaKazi grew from EldoHub’s Digital Apprenticeship Programme, piloted in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic with support from the UK–Kenya Tech Hub and the Africa Technology and Innovation Partnerships programme.
The initiative addressed two urgent needs: young technology professionals required practical experience to transition into decent work, while SMEs needed affordable, reliable digital support to adapt and grow.
Junior professionals were recruited and assessed through online tests, case studies and interviews. Successful candidates were matched with organisations to deliver projects in web development, data analysis, digital marketing, chatbot creation, payment integration and other areas. Participants also received employability training and one-to-one mentorship.
The results showed that a structured connection between skills and real work could benefit talent and businesses. SasaKazi—meaning “now work”—was developed as a digital platform to scale this model.
Today, SasaKazi is evolving into a technology-enabled BPO and managed digital-services company serving African and international markets.
Our Vision
To build an inclusive digital economy where African talent can compete globally and businesses can access the skills they need to grow.
Our Mission
To connect businesses with skilled and reliable African talent through technology-enabled outsourcing, managed digital services and workforce solutions that create business value and meaningful employment.
Our Social Impact Mission
To unlock Africa’s digital future by providing businesses with top-tier technology talent, supporting young African professionals to secure meaningful digital work and enabling African SMEs to accelerate their digital transformation.
Our Values
Client value: responding to genuine business needs.
Opportunity through merit: allowing professionals to demonstrate capability.
Professional excellence: promoting quality, accountability and ethical conduct.
Inclusion: expanding access for women, youth, persons with disabilities and underserved professionals.
Partnership: connecting employers, mentors, institutions and development actors.
Sustainable impact: using enterprise to create employment and strengthen Africa’s digital economy.
Our Impact
For businesses, SasaKazi improves access to digital skills and delivery capacity. For talent, it creates experience, income, mentorship and career pathways. For the ecosystem, it strengthens the connection between training and market demand.
Historical programme materials report more than 6,000 technology professionals reached, more than 1,000 employers in the database, 386 completed projects, work across several African countries and a reported 90% job-absorption rate following digital apprenticeships.
These figures require verification before public publication.
